The Six Stages

Assess → Design → Invest → Engineer → Stage → Deploy

01

Assess Fixed fee

We embed with your team to diagnose the target workflow, quantify the value at stake, and identify exactly where AI and agentic automation change the economics. You leave this stage with a clear-eyed view of the opportunity.

02

Design Fixed fee

We architect the solution and, just as importantly, the outcome plan and KPI framework — the metrics we'll be measured against, the delivery path, and the proof plan. This is where the promise becomes a plan.

03

Invest The Gate

The stage that makes everything else possible. We ascertain a clear executive sponsor inside your organization and establish a clear line of sight to the budget available for the transformation — all the way through deploy. No build begins until the path to investment is real. This is how we can afford to be paid on outcomes.

04

Engineer Milestone / Outcome

Forward-deployed engineers build the AI and agentic workflows in weeks, drawing on our library of reusable accelerators so each engagement moves faster and de-risks the last.

05

Stage Milestone / Outcome

Every build is validated under production-like conditions — user acceptance testing, security, compliance, and business sign-off — before it ever touches production. Enterprise-grade reliability, verified.

06

Deploy Paid on outcomes

We deploy into production and stay hands-on for 2–4 weeks of handholding. Then we measure. Within 90–120 days, the outcomes we committed to should be landing — and that's when Aozla is fully paid.
