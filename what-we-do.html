What We Do

We re-architect enterprise workflows with AI — and prove the return.

01

Gen AI & Agentic AI

Generative, predictive, analytics, and operational AI — designed, built, and deployed into your live workflows. We work across OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google Gemini, Perplexity, Cursor, and custom tooling, choosing the right model for the job rather than forcing a single stack.

GTM Transformation

We re-architect marketing and sales workflows with AI and agentic automation: pipeline acceleration, stage-conversion, seller and partner enablement, quota and territory modeling, pricing and packaging, and why-win/loss reporting — rebuilt as model-driven, measurable flows.

Procure-to-pay, financial reporting, and back-office processes re-engineered with AI and agentic automation — replacing disconnected, manual steps with measurable, model-driven flows that stand up to enterprise scrutiny.
