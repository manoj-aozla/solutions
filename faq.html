Frequently asked questions

What is Aozla?

Aozla is a Forward Deployed AI Engineering and Transformation Company — an AI and Agentic Design, Implementation and Operations partner enabling a business to scale and create measurable efficiencies in revenue capture, GTM growth, cost to serve, and productivity.

What does Aozla do?

Aozla re-architects complex, high-touch enterprise workflows with AI and agentic automation across GTM, ERP and finance, procurement, and security — delivered by forward-deployed teams and built on reusable AI accelerators.

How does Aozla's pricing and engagement model work?

Aozla assesses and designs for a fixed fee, then delivers in weeks and gets paid on the measurable outcomes the client realizes. Everything beyond design is tied to milestones or to the KPIs agreed up front.

What industries does Aozla serve?

Enterprise and midsize organizations in fast-paced and regulated industries — Technology, Finance, Automotive/Rail, Healthcare, Manufacturing, Retail, and Energy — where security, compliance, cost simplification, and productivity matter most.

What is Aozla's six-stage delivery method?

Assess and Design (fixed fee), Invest (the gate — securing an executive sponsor and line of sight to budget), then Engineer, Stage, and Deploy (tied to milestones and outcomes). Within 90–120 days of go-live, the first planned outcomes should be measurable.

How does Aozla approach security and compliance?

Security by Design and Security in the Design — on-premise, in cloud, or at the edge. As agents, APIs, MCP connections, and non-human identities multiply, Aozla governs and secures every new attack surface, covering vulnerability assurance, agent access and identity, prompt-injection defense, regulatory compliance, and AI governance.

What is Aozla RunOps?

RunOps is Aozla's Day-2 operations service — a separate, recurring engagement in which Aozla operates, monitors, and continuously optimizes live AI workloads so value keeps compounding after launch. Contracts are annual and renewable.
